Common questions

Before purchasing a Sefer Torah

How much does a new Sefer Torah cost?

Pricing depends on the script, size, workmanship, inspection requirements and accompanying accessories. Please request a quotation for your specific needs.

What is the difference between Ashkenazi and Sephardi Torah scrolls?

Communities follow different scribal traditions and customs. Confirm the appropriate script and tradition for the synagogue where the scroll will be used.

Can I inquire about a Torah scroll dedicated in someone's memory?

Yes. You can ask about purchasing a Sefer Torah for a memorial dedication. Details and timing should be discussed directly with the business.

Are completed Torah scrolls in stock?

Please ask the gallery about current availability. This website does not provide live inventory.

Can I visit the gallery in Jerusalem?

Blotnik Gallery is at 30 Shilo Street in Nachlaot, Jerusalem. We recommend arranging your visit in advance once the business contact details are confirmed.
